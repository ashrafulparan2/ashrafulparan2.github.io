<p align="justify">
I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at <a href="https://www.uiu.ac.bd/">United International University</a> (UIU), Bangladesh. I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the <a href="https://www.cuet.ac.bd/">Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET)</a>, graduating summa cum laude and ranking 2nd in my class. Previously, I served as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at <a href="https://www.seu.edu.bd/">Southeast University</a>.
</p> 

<p align="justify">
My research focuses on <b>Natural Language Processing (NLP)</b>, <b>Large Language Models (LLMs)</b>, and <b>Multimodal Learning</b>, with a particular emphasis on building intelligent systems that integrate text, image, audio, and video understanding for low-resource languages such as Bengali. To this end, I develop methods for multimodal fusion and content moderation <span class="cites" tabindex="0">[1&ndash;2]<span class="cites__pop">
    <a class="cites__item" href="https://aclanthology.org/2025.dravidianlangtech-1.86.pdf"><span class="cites__n">1</span><span class="cites__t">Multimodal Fusion for Hate Speech Detection: A Transformer-Based Approach with Cross-Modal Attention<span class="cites__v">DravidianLangTech@NAACL 2025</span></span></a>
    <a class="cites__item" href="https://aclanthology.org/2024.semeval-1.178/"><span class="cites__n">2</span><span class="cites__t">Finetuning Sentence Transformer to Find Semantic Textual Relatedness<span class="cites__v">SemEval 2024</span></span></a>
  </span></span>, leverage large language models to estimate check-worthiness and combat misinformation <span class="cites" tabindex="0">[3&ndash;5]<span class="cites__pop">
    <a class="cites__item" href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11022535/"><span class="cites__n">3</span><span class="cites__t">Leveraging Large Language Models to Estimate Check-Worthiness of Multilingual Tweets<span class="cites__v">ICCIT 2024 &middot; Best Paper Award</span></span></a>
    <a class="cites__item" href="https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3740/paper-58.pdf"><span class="cites__n">4</span><span class="cites__t">Pre-trained Transformer-based Approach to Detect Check-Worthy Tweets<span class="cites__v">CLEF 2024</span></span></a>
    <a class="cites__item" href="https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3740/paper-50.pdf"><span class="cites__n">5</span><span class="cites__t">Finding Subjectivity in News Articles using Llama<span class="cites__v">CLEF 2024</span></span></a>
  </span></span>, and investigate cross-dialect intent classification <span class="cites" tabindex="0">[6]<span class="cites__pop">
    <a class="cites__item" href="https://aclanthology.org/2024.arabicnlp-1.38/"><span class="cites__n">6</span><span class="cites__t">Classification of Cross-Dialect Intent in the Banking Domain using Transformers<span class="cites__v">ArabicNLP 2024</span></span></a>
  </span></span>.
</p> 

<p align="justify">
I have served as a reviewer for <b>ACL Rolling Review (ARR)</b>, <b>NAACL</b>, <b>CLEF</b>, and <b>SemEval</b>, contributing to the peer-review process of high-impact NLP conferences and workshops.
</p>

<p align="justify">
I am passionate about teaching and mentoring students in areas such as Machine Learning, Operating Systems, and Data Mining. My broader interests include open-source development, competitive programming (1200+ solved problems across Codeforces, UVA, LightOJ, and AtCoder), and community building within the Bangladeshi research ecosystem.
</p>
